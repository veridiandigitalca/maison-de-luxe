Maison / Process

From first thought
to final reveal.

Every commission begins with an idea. The process gives that idea room to become something considered, personal and beautifully made.

01 / THE VISION

Start with the feeling.

Share your occasion, preferred date, references, colours, mood boards or any details you already know.

02 / THE DESIGN

Refine the direction.

The creative direction is shaped around the information and references you provide.

03 / THE MAKING

Made by hand.

The chosen direction becomes a finished confection, floral arrangement or signature creation.

The final step

Then comes
the moment.

Pickup and local delivery options can be discussed during the commission process. Fragile, handcrafted pieces deserve thoughtful handling from the Maison to their destination.
