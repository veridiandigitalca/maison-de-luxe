A boutique studio for

Maison De Luxe is a Mississauga-based custom design studio bringing together the worlds of premium pastry artistry and elegant floral design.

The philosophy

Bringing your vision to life.

Maison De Luxe is built around a simple idea: the most meaningful pieces should feel like they belong to the person and the moment they were made for.

Clients may arrive with an inspiration image, a colour palette, a mood board, a theme or only a feeling. The studio uses those starting points to develop bespoke confections, florals and hybrid creations.

The atelier mindset
Every detail has a reason.
Every commission has a story.

The Maison's visual language lives in the details: colour, proportion, texture, flowers, piping, presentation and the feeling created when everything arrives together.

Based in Mississauga

Created in the GTA,
for the moments that matter.

Maison De Luxe primarily operates from Mississauga and serves the broader Greater Toronto Area through scheduled custom orders.
